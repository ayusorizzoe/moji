      how moji works

      ➕ add

      open 🛠️, tap ➕, pick an emoji, then tap the canvas. the new emoji is selected and ready to edit.

      tap ➕ again for another of the same. tap the emoji in the tab under ➕ to pick a different one.

      👆 select

      👆🙂→🙂

      tap an emoji to select it. tap empty space to deselect.

      where emoji overlap, tap the same spot again to reach the one underneath.

      ✋ move, resize, rotate

      drag a selected emoji to move it. put two fingers on it and pinch or turn to resize and rotate.

      with a mouse: drag the corner dot to resize, the top dot to rotate.

      👥 several at once

      rest one finger on empty space and tap other emoji with another finger. with a mouse, shift-click.

      then tap the selection for 🫂 group; grouped emoji move together. ✂️ ungroup splits them again.

      💬 actions

      tap a selected emoji again for its menu:

      • 🟦🟦 duplicate
      • ⏫ to front · 🔼 forward
      • 🔽 backward · ⏬ to back

      🗂️ layers

      everything you placed, front to back. tap a row to select it.

      the eye hides an emoji; the lock keeps it from being moved by accident. drag ≡ to restack, or into and out of groups.

      🖐️ canvas

      pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to look around. with a mouse, scroll to zoom and drag to pan. 🎯 brings the canvas back.

      tap the canvas label (or its small icon in the corner) for size, shape and background color.

      ⏳ history

      ↩️ undo and ↪️ redo. the strip at the bottom fills up as you work: drag it to look back, ▶️ to replay.

      🌱 starts a new version from an earlier moment; 🌿 lists them. reset starts over.

      ✅ export

      ✅ in 🛠️ saves a png of the canvas. anything outside the canvas is left out.

      ⌨️ keyboard

      ⌘C copy · ⌘V paste · ⌘D duplicate · esc deselect (ctrl on windows)
