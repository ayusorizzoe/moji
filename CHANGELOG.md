# changelog

all notable changes to moji are documented in this file.
the newest revision appears first.

## 0.0.2 — 2026-11-25

refinements from testing 0.0.1 on an iphone (a fix for portrait layout drift, delete and placement controls moved into the tools panel, delete all, button name captions, a friendlier canvas panel), followed by editing existing artwork (selection, moving, resizing, rotating, multiple selection, duplication, grouping, stacking order) and an interactive timeline with playback and branching history.

### added

- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: a drop-down tab that grows down out of the tools panel under the active tool. while placing, it hangs under ➕ and shows the current emoji (tap it to change emoji); while deleting, it hangs under ➖ and holds 🧹 (delete all). tapping ➕ or ➖ again, tapping any other tool (🎯, ✅, ↩️, ↪️, or the other of ➕/➖), or collapsing 🛠️ folds it back up and ends the mode; the tab only ever belongs to the tool in use. tapping a dimmed tool changes nothing.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the tools panel and its drop-down tab are one continuous glass shape that genuinely morphs. each frame, one svg path is rebuilt from three springs (panel width, tab depth, tab position) and used as the glass clip (`clip-path: path()`), the soft shadow, and the hairline edge. the tab grows out of the panel's bottom edge with smooth inward curves where they meet, or rises straight into the panel's rounded end when it sits at the edge (under ➕); switching between ➕ and ➖ slides the tab across; collapsing folds the tab up before the panel narrows, and expanding widens the panel before the tab grows. reduced motion jumps straight to the final shape.
- `js/main.js`: delete all (🧹), which removes every emoji, inside and outside the canvas, as one step (`clear-objects` in the action history). ↩️ restores everything; it is saved and restored like other edits, and 🧹 is dimmed when there is nothing to delete.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: a short caption that names the button just tapped (for example "delete", "delete all", "undo", "change emoji") right next to it: just under a toolbar button (moved beside the drop-down tab when the tab is there), beside the tab's own button, and above ⏳. it fades after about a second, names the button only without explaining it, and is hidden from screen readers, which use the buttons' own labels.
- `css/styles.css`: `--color-accent` (warm emoji yellow, `#FFD43B`) for primary actions, and `--font-family-numeric` (the system's rounded face, sf rounded on apple devices, the system ui face elsewhere) for numbers typed into fields.

- `js/history.js` (new): the history engine, separate from the interface. each branch is an ordered log of events (an edit, an undo, or a redo), so undo and redo stay visible as part of the creative process. the artwork and its undo/redo stacks at any moment are rebuilt by replaying events from the project's base state, with in-memory snapshots every 32 events for fast scrubbing (never saved). a branch stores only its own events plus the point it started from, so shared history is not copied. operations: `add-objects`, `delete-objects`, `transform-objects`, `set-groups`, `reorder`, plus the 0.0.1 types (`add-object`, `delete-object`, `clear-objects`, `resize-canvas`) so older projects replay unchanged. it also handles validation, saving, migration and the before/after transitions used by playback.
- `js/main.js`: selection as the default canvas behavior whenever ➕ and ➖ are off, with no selection button. tapping an emoji selects it (nothing moves, stacking is unchanged) and shows a subtle dashed outline that follows its rotated bounds; tapping empty space deselects; tapping another emoji switches. repeated taps on about the same spot (within 18px) cycle through overlapping emoji from the top down and back to the top. hit testing accounts for position, size, rotation, zoom, pan and stacking order, uses the same stage coordinates as rendering, and works for emoji outside the canvas.
- `js/main.js`: editing. a long press (about 0.4s) on any emoji selects it and starts editing at once; keep moving to drag it. once editing, dragging is immediate and releasing keeps editing on. editing ends when you tap empty space, select another emoji, or switch to ➕ or ➖.
- `js/main.js`: uniform resizing and rotation of the selection: a corner handle resizes, a handle above the outline rotates, and two fingers on the selection resize and rotate together. handles have 44px touch targets and the outline never draws smaller than 44px on screen, so tiny emoji stay manageable without changing their size. each completed drag, resize or rotation is one undoable `transform-objects` step; intermediate movement is not recorded.
- `js/main.js`: multiple selection without a selection tool. desktop: shift-click adds or removes an emoji; a plain click selects one; clicking empty space clears. touch: rest one finger on empty space and tap emoji with another finger to add or remove them; lifting the first finger keeps the selection. two fingers that move together still pan and zoom. a multiple selection shares one outline; a long press on any selected emoji edits all of them, and dragging, resizing and rotating act on the whole selection around its shared center while keeping each emoji's relative position, size and rotation.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: an object menu (compact frosted glass, near the selection, kept inside the screen) that opens when you tap the selection while editing; it never opens on its own. it shows only actions that would do something: duplicate, group, ungroup, to front, forward, backward, to back. opening 🛠️ or tapping elsewhere closes it without clearing the selection.
- `js/main.js`: duplicate copies the selected emoji with new ids, a small offset, and all other properties, placed directly above the originals (grouped originals become a new group).
- `js/main.js`: groups. grouping gives the selected emoji a shared `groupId`; selecting any member selects the whole group, which edits as one unit while each emoji keeps its own properties. ungrouping removes the `groupId` and leaves positions, sizes, rotations and stacking unchanged.
- `js/main.js`: stacking order: bring forward and send backward move the selection one step (another group counts as one step), bring to front and send to back move it to the top or bottom. coordinates, size and rotation never change, and the order is used for drawing, hit testing and export.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: an interactive timeline. ⏳ grows into a panel with a status line, a scrubber (a tick per event, undo and redo in amber, green dots where this branch started from another), ▶️ play/⏸️ pause, a speed button (0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×), 🌱 start from here, and 🌿 view branches. while open it takes priority: placing, deleting, editing and the object menu are suspended, outlines and handles hide, 🛠️ and the canvas label rest dimmed, and canvas taps never change the artwork (zoom and pan still work). closing returns to the latest state of the active branch and restores the previous mode and selection where they still exist.
- `js/main.js`: scrubbing rebuilds the artwork at any moment instantly (pointer or arrow keys) without touching the live artwork, which is what gets saved and exported.
- `js/main.js`: playback replays the active branch's whole process, mistakes and corrections included, at a steady rhythm. each step interpolates only what that event changed: new emoji fade and grow in, removed emoji fade out, moved, resized and rotated emoji travel smoothly, and canvas size changes animate; everything else stays still. pausing finishes the current step. reduced motion shows each step without animation.
- `js/main.js`: branching. 🌱 appears while previewing an earlier moment and starts a new branch there (named branch 1, branch 2, …), which becomes active with the selection cleared; the original branch and its later history are kept. 🌿 lists branches to switch (restoring that branch's latest artwork), rename (names persist), or delete (with a confirmation; the active branch and the last branch cannot be deleted). up to five branches per project; a sixth is refused with an explanation and nothing is deleted automatically. deleting a branch that others started from hands its shared history to them, so they stay intact.

### changed

- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the bottom-right mode bar (➕ ✖️ while placing, ✖️ while deleting) is removed; every tool now lives in the top-right panel.
- `js/main.js`: ➕ no longer collapses the tools panel when opening the picker, choosing an emoji expands the panel so its drop-down tab is visible, and tapping ➕ while placing stops placing.
- `css/styles.css`: the canvas panel's width and height numbers use the rounded numeric face instead of atkinson hyperlegible, and apply is a warm yellow pill instead of near-black.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: while the drop-down tab overlaps the `canvas` label (on narrow screens), the label fades out and returns when the tab folds away; it never moves.
- `js/main.js`, `js/history.js`: autosave now stores the whole project (all branches with their events and names, the active branch, the project base, plus a safety copy of the current artwork) and never a timeline preview. on startup the project is rebuilt by replaying history and must reproduce the safety copy; if history cannot be read, the artwork is kept, the raw data is backed up under `moji.project.unrestored`, and a new history starts with a message. if storage fills up, saving pauses with a message instead of dropping branches or history.
- `js/history.js`: 0.0.1 projects are migrated on first load. the original data is kept under `moji.project.v1-backup`; its edits become the original branch, followed by one undo per edit beyond the saved cursor, which reproduces the same artwork and the same undo/redo stacks. 0.0.1 did not record when undos happened, so imported history shows each saved edit once. a 0.0.1 project saved without history keeps its artwork as the starting point.
- `js/main.js`: the 0.0.1 undo history (one list and a cursor) is replaced by the branch event log; ↩️ and ↪️ behave as before and are recorded as events.
- `js/main.js`: objects are never changed in place; every edit builds new objects, so history snapshots can share them safely.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`: ⏳ is now a working control (previously a placeholder).

### fixed

- `js/main.js`: on iphone in portrait (found in firefox; every ios browser uses apple's webkit engine), the canvas, the canvas label, placed emoji, taps, and zoom could drift apart vertically and emoji could look stretched. the stage's pixel buffer was only resized on window `resize` events, but its height follows the dynamic viewport (`100dvh`), which changes as the browser toolbars show and hide without always firing `resize`. the stage now tracks its own size with a `ResizeObserver` (plus `visualViewport` and orientation events) and re-checks its size before every frame, so the buffer always matches what is on screen.
- `js/main.js`: touch, mouse, and wheel positions are now measured relative to the stage itself rather than the window.
- `css/styles.css`: ➖ no longer keeps a pressed circle while delete mode is on. the circle nearly filled the 48px toolbar, so it looked off-center, and it was the only button with a lasting highlight. ➕ and ➖ show their mode through the drop-down tab instead.

## 0.0.1 — 2026-11-23

initial visual interface, extended into a functional editor: canvas, zoom and pan, emoji picker and placement, delete, undo/redo, recenter, png export, and automatic local saving.

### added

- `index.html`: document structure with mobile viewport settings (`viewport-fit=cover`), page title, and a visually hidden `moji` heading.
- `index.html`: Atkinson Hyperlegible (regular and bold) loaded from Google Fonts, with a system sans-serif fallback.
- `index.html`: workspace layer and a `<canvas>` stage drawn by `js/main.js`; `js/emoji-data.js` and `js/main.js` load in that order with `defer`; theme color set to the workspace gray.
- `index.html`: settings button (upper left) with a ⚙️ emoji icon and a right-pointing chevron suggesting future rightward expansion.
- `index.html`: tools button (upper right) with 🛠️ and a small chevron, expanding into a row of ➕ add, ➖ delete, ↩️ undo, ↪️ redo, 🎯 recenter, and ✅ export.
- `index.html`: compact timeline placeholder (bottom center) with a ⏳ emoji icon and an empty track; no history entries shown.
- `index.html`: `canvas` label (frosted glass, text) that follows the canvas's upper-left edge at a constant screen size.
- `index.html`: mode bar (bottom right) shown only during placement (➕ to reopen the picker, ✖️ to exit) or delete mode (✖️ to exit).
- `index.html`: canvas properties panel, emoji picker sheet, and a transient status message for export and autosave problems.
- `index.html`: emoji icons and chevrons marked decorative; controls have accessible names; ⚙️ and ⏳ are marked `aria-disabled="true"` because they are not yet functional.
- `css/styles.css`: design tokens as CSS custom properties for colors, glass material, blur, grid, spacing, safe areas, control dimensions, sheet heights, radii, motion, and z-index.
- `css/styles.css`: typography tokens (font family, sizes, weights, line heights, letter spacing), including a `--font-family-emoji` stack (Apple Color Emoji, Segoe UI Emoji, Noto Color Emoji, Twemoji Mozilla).
- `css/styles.css`: global reset that prevents page scrolling and overscroll.
- `css/styles.css`: workspace background (`#D8DADC`) with a subtle 1px grid: secondary lines (`#D0D2D4`) and primary lines (`#C5C8CB`) every fifth line; the grid sits outside the canvas.
- `css/styles.css`: translucent glass material for all floating controls and panels: about 70% opaque light fill (80% for panels), 22px blur with increased saturation, a hairline edge and top highlight instead of borders, and soft layered shadows.
- `css/styles.css`: capsule-shaped controls 48px tall with 44px emoji touch targets; panels with 22–30px radii; pressed states that scale controls down slightly.
- `css/styles.css`: tools morph from a capsule into a wider capsule expanding to the left (340ms spring curve); 🛠️ stays pinned in place, the chevron rotates to show whether the panel will expand or collapse, and tools fade and slide in one after another, nearest first.
- `css/styles.css`: on narrow screens where the expanded tools reach the settings corner, ⚙️ fades aside while the tools are open and returns when they close; it never moves.
- `css/styles.css`: canvas properties as a compact popover with a segmented aspect-ratio control and inline width × height fields.
- `css/styles.css`: emoji picker as a floating sheet rising from the bottom, about a third of the screen when compact and expandable to just below the top controls; a search capsule with an icon and muted category icons share one row so the grid keeps most of the space.
- `css/styles.css`: floating control positioning anchored to corners and bottom center, respecting device safe areas, with responsive edge spacing and timeline width at 600px and 1024px.
- `css/styles.css`: visible keyboard focus outline (`:focus-visible`).
- `css/styles.css`: opaque fallback when `backdrop-filter` is unsupported or reduced transparency is preferred; reduced-motion support that removes movement and keeps only quick fades; borders for forced-colors mode.
- `js/main.js`: document model in which every placed emoji is an independent object (id, emoji, render style, position in artwork coordinates, size, rotation, creation order, timestamp); array order is the stacking order. coordinates are centered on the canvas so canvas resizing never moves objects.
- `js/main.js`: action history of immutable, self-contained entries (`add-object`, `delete-object`, `resize-canvas`) that can each be applied and reverted; undo and redo move a cursor through the list, and a new action discards the redo branch.
- `js/main.js`: default 9:16 canvas (1080 × 1920 artwork px, `#FFFFFF`), fitted and centered between the top and bottom controls, with edges snapped to device pixels.
- `js/main.js`: zoom and pan that change only the view, never the artwork. touch: one finger taps, two fingers pan, pinch zooms. desktop: drag (left or middle button) pans; mouse wheel, trackpad scrolling, and trackpad pinch all zoom around the pointer. every wheel event zooms, instead of guessing whether it came from a mouse or a trackpad.
- `js/main.js`: stage rendering that redraws the white canvas and all objects from the document model each frame at the device pixel ratio, using the platform's native color emoji font; objects outside the canvas stay visible on the workspace.
- `js/main.js`: workspace grid kept aligned with the artwork while zooming and panning, with spacing adapting to stay visually quiet.
- `js/main.js`: tap detection that ignores pinches, multi-finger gestures, and drags, so gestures never place or delete emoji.
- `js/main.js`: tools panel behavior: 🛠️ toggles the panel; ➕ closes it and opens the picker; ➖ toggles delete mode and keeps the panel open; ↩️ and ↪️ undo and redo without closing the panel (dimmed when unavailable); 🎯 and ✅ act immediately without changing the current mode.
- `js/main.js`: 🎯 recenter, which smoothly returns the view to the fitted, centered canvas (instantly when reduced motion is preferred); it changes only the view and creates no history entry.
- `js/emoji-data.js`: the emoji catalog (1,369 emoji in nine categories, each with a searchable name), kept as a data-only file separate from the application logic in `js/main.js`; `js/main.js` reads it from `window.MOJI_EMOJI_CATEGORIES`.
- `js/main.js`: emoji picker with search (by unicode name), category navigation that follows scrolling, and a scrollable grid of 1,369 emoji (unicode 13.0 and earlier) across nine categories; selecting an emoji closes the picker and enters placement mode without placing anything.
- `js/main.js`: placement mode, where each tap places a new copy of the selected emoji at the tapped artwork position.
- `js/main.js`: delete mode, where each tap removes only the topmost emoji under the pointer (with a minimum touch radius for small emoji).
- `js/main.js`: canvas properties opened from the canvas label: aspect ratio presets (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 3:4, 16:9) and custom width and height (64–4096 px); resizing is symmetric around the center, undoable, and never moves, scales, rotates, or removes objects. the popover opens above the label when there is room.
- `js/main.js`: one-tap png export: ✅ renders the png from the document model at the canvas's own pixel size, independent of zoom, and starts the browser's download directly, with no preview or dialog. only the canvas area is drawn, so the grid, interface, label, and objects outside the canvas are excluded and objects crossing the edge are cropped. failures show a brief message.
- `js/main.js`: automatic local saving of the structured project (canvas, objects, and undo/redo history) to `localStorage`, about 0.4s after each change (placement, deletion, canvas resize, undo, redo) and when the page is hidden. if storage is full, the artwork is saved without history.
- `js/main.js`: automatic restore on startup, validated before use and without creating history entries; damaged history is dropped while the artwork is kept. a project that cannot be restored is copied to a backup key (`moji.project.unrestored`) before anything new is saved; if no backup can be made, autosave pauses for the session.
- `js/main.js`: escape key closes an open panel, then exits the current mode, then collapses the tools panel; ios page-zoom gestures and the stage context menu are suppressed.
- `README.md`: project description, how to run it, current features, emoji rendering notes, and a note that local browser storage is not a backup.
- `CHANGELOG.md`: changelog structure and this entry.
