# changelog

all notable changes to moji are documented in this file.
the newest revision appears first.

## 0.0.4 — in progress

seamless placement and immediate editing, from testing 0.0.3 on an iphone: placing an emoji no longer leaves ➕ on (stray taps made unwanted copies), one tap now gives full editing (move, resize, rotate, the menu) with no long press, reset starts the whole project over, and the picker gains the emoji 14.0 and 15.0 sets.

### added

- `js/emoji-data.js`: 57 newer emoji from emoji 14.0 and 15.0 in the picker, each in its matching category and searchable by its unicode name, for example 🫧 bubbles, 🫠 melting face, 🥹 face holding back tears, 🫡 saluting face, 🫶 heart hands, 🩷 pink heart, 🩵 light blue heart, 🩶 grey heart, 🪸 coral, 🪷 lotus, 🪿 goose, 🫎 moose, 🪼 jellyfish, 🐦‍⬛ black bird, 🫘 beans, 🫛 pea pod, 🪩 mirror ball, 🪭 folding hand fan and 🛜 wireless. existing entries are unchanged. these need ios 16.4 or later (15.4 for the emoji 14.0 ones) or a similarly recent android or windows; older systems may draw them as empty boxes, on the canvas and in exports.
- `js/main.js`: keyboard shortcuts on desktop: ⌘C / ctrl+c copies the selected emoji, ⌘V / ctrl+v pastes them, ⌘D / ctrl+d duplicates them (the same as duplicate in the object menu, instead of the browser's bookmark shortcut). the clipboard is moji's own and lasts until the page is reloaded; it keeps the emoji as they were when copied, so a paste works even after the originals are moved or deleted, or on another branch. each paste adds a fresh copy on top of the stack, a step further down and to the right each time, with new ids and new groups (a copied group pastes as a new group), selects it, and is one undo step ("paste" in the timeline). hidden and locked emoji are never copied. while typing in a field (search, sizes, branch names) the browser's own copy and paste work as usual, and the shortcuts do nothing while the timeline is open or during a drag.

### changed

- `js/main.js`: placing is one emoji at a time. tapping the canvas while placing adds one emoji (one undo step), ends ➕ (its tab folds away), and selects the new emoji, ready to move, resize, rotate or open its menu with no further tap or long press. further taps on the canvas no longer add copies.
- `js/main.js`: ➕ remembers the last emoji chosen. with one remembered, tapping ➕ starts placing it straight away (the tab under ➕ shows it; tap the tab to choose another in the picker); with none yet, ➕ opens the picker as before. tapping ➕ again while placing still cancels. the memory lasts until the page is reloaded.
- `js/main.js`: a selected emoji is fully editable; there is no separate editing state behind a long press. one tap selects an emoji, and it can then be dragged with one finger, resized and rotated with two fingers that start on it, or (with a mouse) resized and rotated with the box's handles, which now show as soon as it is selected. on touch screens the shape outline is white while selected and turns blue only while it is being moved, resized or rotated, returning to white when you let go. a long press on an unselected emoji still selects it and lets the same press drag it. selecting never adds a history step; each completed move, resize or rotation is still one undo step.
- `js/main.js`: the object menu opens without a long press: tap the selected emoji again to open it, again to close it. tapping elsewhere and starting a move, resize or rotation still close it, and it never opens on its own. where other emoji overlap the selected one, a tap there still steps to the next emoji underneath (overlap cycling takes priority); tap a part of the selected emoji that nothing covers, or empty space inside a multiple selection's outline, for the menu.
- `js/main.js`: escape now clears the selection directly (there is no editing state to leave first).
- `js/main.js`: reset (in the open timeline) now starts the whole project over instead of keeping the artwork: every emoji, step and branch is erased, and the canvas returns to the default 9:16 black and is recentered. it still asks once ("sure?") and cannot be undone. the remembered ➕ emoji is kept.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: timeline messages (the reset confirmation and the branch limit) are no longer cut off on phones: a message may wrap onto a second line in a slightly smaller size, while the usual status stays on one line. both messages are also shorter: "tap sure? to erase every emoji, step and branch" and "5 branches is the limit. delete one in 🌿 first". checked down to a 320px-wide screen.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: a check of every text on phone screens (320, 375 and 430px wide) fixed the rest:
  - the branch list no longer grows wider than its panel when a branch has a long name. before, ✏️ and 🗑️ were pushed out of sight on every phone. names now take up to two lines, then end in "…".
  - layer names in the layers panel take up to two lines in a slightly smaller size instead of being cut to a few letters. the panel is a little wider on phones (up to 280px, at most 78% of the screen), and its row controls are slightly narrower. emoji not in the catalog show as "emoji" instead of repeating the emoji.
  - the timeline status wraps onto a second line for long branch names instead of being cut off.
  - on the narrowest phones (under 360px wide) the seven tools are 34px, so ➕ is no longer pushed off the left edge when 🛠️ is open.
  - the branch list says "1 step" instead of "1 steps".
  - the reset button is named "reset everything" (its caption and screen reader label), since it now erases everything.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the name caption for buttons in the open timeline (▶️, speed, 🌿, 🌱, reset) now appears just above the whole timeline. before, it was placed inside the open panel, over the slider, and drawn behind the panel, so it showed as unreadable text behind it (most visible on desktop after clicking reset). captions now always draw in front of the controls.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: text that comes out of a shape now lines up with that shape's edge instead of centering on the button:
  - timeline button captions (▶️, speed, 🌿, 🌱, reset, ⏳) sit just above the timeline, flush with its left edge for buttons on its left half and its right edge for the rest. the ⏳ caption waits until the timeline finishes opening or closing, since its edges move while it morphs.
  - tool captions sit under the tools panel, flush with its right edge (the same edge the layers panel lines up with), and drop below the ➕ / ➖ tab if it is in the way.
  - the branch list is now exactly as wide as the open timeline, so its edges line up with the timeline below it.
- `js/history.js`: timeline playback now turns emoji around the right point. when several emoji are rotated or resized together (a group or a multiple selection), each one swings along its arc around their shared center, as it did while editing. before, each one slid in a straight line from start to end while turning in place, so mid-animation they cut across the middle and the turn looked like it was around the wrong axis (the final result was always correct). playback finds that center from the recorded before and after positions, so it also works for steps recorded before this fix. a single emoji still moves straight while turning in place.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: 🤔 help replaces ⚙️ settings in the upper-left corner. it opens a compact, scrollable guide in the same frosted glass, with short instructions and small emoji examples for adding and selecting emoji, moving, resizing and rotating, multiple selection and groups, the object menu, layers, canvas navigation, undo, redo and the timeline, exporting, and the desktop shortcuts. ✕, escape, a tap on the canvas, or opening another panel or the timeline closes it, and opening or closing it never changes the artwork or the selection.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the upper-left corner while the canvas fills the screen. the round canvas button (with its miniature of the canvas's shape and color) takes the corner, and 🤔 slides beside it at 75% size, its top edge level with the button's top, without overlapping anything (on page load it takes its place without sliding). the smaller 🤔 keeps a touch target of about 44px or more that never reaches over the canvas button. zoomed out, 🤔 is full size in the corner again. once you zoom or pan so there is room above the canvas, the button morphs back into the `canvas` label above the canvas and 🤔 slides back into the corner. both open the same panels as before. on narrow phones both step aside while 🛠️ is open, as before.

### removed

- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: ⚙️ settings. it was a placeholder since 0.0.1 with no preferences behind it (tapping it only showed its name), so nothing had to move: canvas size, aspect ratio and background color are already in canvas properties, emoji choice is in the picker, and there are no workspace grid controls to keep. no replacement settings panel was added.

## 0.0.3 — in progress

making moji feel like a phone app rather than a website, following instagram stories: selection that hugs each emoji's shape on touch screens, controls that step aside while you move things, a canvas that fills the screen, background colors (black by default), no accidental text selection, a timeline you can always see, a way to start the history over, a layers panel with visibility, locking and group folders, a mask for artwork outside the canvas, and dragging selected emoji without a long press.

### added

- `js/main.js`: on touch screens the selection follows each emoji's own shape: a white outline while selected, blue while editing, with no box and no handles (drag with one finger; resize and rotate with two). a mouse still gets the box with resize and rotate handles. the style follows the last input used, so a laptop with a touch screen switches as you go. each emoji in a multiple selection gets its own outline.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the timeline is always visible. the closed ⏳ capsule shows a live strip that gains a tick with every change (its marker gives a small pulse), so you can watch the history grow while you work. dragging or tapping the strip opens the timeline at that moment; after the first touch the playhead follows your finger's movement, so it never jumps while the strip widens. with many steps, each tick stands for a few neighboring events (amber if any of them is an undo or redo). while open, a plain tap anywhere outside it closes it, just like ⏳; dragging or pinching outside still pans and zooms.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: reset in the open timeline erases every step and every branch and makes the current artwork the new starting point (it is not changed). it asks once more ("sure?", in red, for about four seconds) and cannot be undone. to start from a blank canvas, delete all with 🧹 first, then reset.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: while an emoji is being moved, resized or rotated, all controls fade away and come back when you let go.
- `js/main.js`: on phones (a screen whose shorter side is under 600px) the canvas fills the whole screen behind the controls, like a story: it covers the screen when that crops only a little (9:16 on a tall phone) and otherwise fits the full width or height (1:1, 16:9) so nothing important is cut off. pinch to zoom out and see the whole canvas; 🎯 returns to the full-screen view. larger screens keep the fit between the top and bottom controls.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: while the canvas reaches up behind the top controls, the `canvas` label morphs into a round button beside ⚙️, the same size as ⚙️, showing a miniature of the canvas (its shape and background color, updated as they change). once you zoom out far enough that there is room above the canvas, it morphs back into the label just above the canvas (the switch animates; following the canvas does not). the outside label now stays on screen when the canvas's left edge is panned away, and the round button steps aside with ⚙️ when the expanded tools need that corner.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`, `js/history.js`: background colors in the canvas panel (tap the `canvas` label): eleven simple swatches (black, white, gray, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, brown) and a color wheel that opens the system color picker for any other color. a swatch applies at once; the wheel previews live while you pick and records one step when you're done (or when the panel closes). the chosen color shows a ring, and no caption names it. each change is one undoable `set-background` step, appears in the timeline as "background", is blended smoothly during playback, is saved, and is used for the png export.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: a layers panel. 🗂️ in the tools opens a floating frosted-glass list of every emoji and group in stacking order, frontmost first, under the tools on the right. it stays open until closed with its ✕ (or escape): selecting, moving, resizing and rotating on the canvas, collapsing 🛠️, and canvas taps leave it open, and opening it never changes the selection. compact and scrollable on phones (at most about 40% of the screen height), taller and wider on larger screens; it fades with the other controls while you move an emoji. each row has the emoji, its name (from the emoji catalog), a visibility toggle, a lock toggle, and a grip for dragging. the selection is highlighted and stays in sync both ways: tapping a row selects that emoji (shift, ⌘ or ctrl adds and removes on desktop), and selecting on the canvas highlights its rows.
- `js/main.js`, `js/history.js`: visibility and locking as saved, undoable layer properties (`hidden`, `locked` on each emoji, changed by a new `set-flags` step). hidden emoji are not drawn and never export, but stay in the panel (dimmed, with a crossed eye). locked emoji stay visible but canvas taps, drags, delete mode, 🧹 delete all, and selection pass through them; the panel can still unlock them. a toggle on a group row applies to all of its members (a mixed group turns fully on). 🧹 now keeps locked and hidden emoji.
- `js/main.js`: groups are folders in the layers panel. collapsed, a group row shows up to three of its emoji fanned and "group · n"; tapping it selects the whole group. the chevron expands it to show its members, indented; tapping a member selects just that emoji without ungrouping, and members keep their own visibility and lock. expanding and collapsing is an interface preference only (never a timeline step, not saved).
- `js/main.js`, `js/history.js`: drag a row's grip to restack it or reorganize groups: move an emoji or a whole group up or down, drop an emoji on a group row to put it into that group (top of the group), between members to place it there, or outside a group to take it out; members can be reordered and moved between groups. a blue line shows where it will land, indented inside a group; dropping into a group highlights that group's row. the canvas follows live while you drag; the list scrolls when you hold near its top or bottom edge; the selection is kept. the whole drag becomes one undoable `arrange` step (new stacking order plus any membership changes); cancelling (escape, or the browser cancelling the touch) restores everything with no step. groups never go inside groups, so circular groups cannot happen, and a group left with one member dissolves in the same step. groups are flat and all positions are absolute, so changing membership never moves, scales or rotates an emoji.
- `js/main.js`: the canvas overflow mask. emoji are drawn in two passes: inside the canvas at full strength, outside it faintly. while the canvas fills the screen (the fitted phone view or closer) anything outside it is fully hidden and out of reach of taps; as you zoom out it fades in to a faint 30% over the workspace, so you can still see, select and move emoji that sit outside the export area. it is drawing only: no layer, no history step, positions untouched, selection outlines and handles still drawn in full, and exports still crop exactly to the canvas.
- `index.html`: home screen meta tags, so moji added to the home screen opens full screen without browser bars where the browser supports it.

### changed

- `js/main.js`: an emoji that is already selected now moves as soon as you drag it, with no long press (it stays selected; a plain tap still cycles through overlapping emoji, and a drag never switches to another emoji underneath). an unselected emoji still selects on tap and moves after a long press. dragging any emoji of a multiple selection moves them all. each drag is one undo step.
- `js/main.js`, `js/history.js`: grouping now also gathers the members into one block in the stacking order, where the topmost of them was, so a group is a single folder in the layers panel (one `arrange` step instead of `set-groups`). groups made before this keep their stacking order; one whose members are apart shows as a folder at its topmost member until it is next moved.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`: the object menu is redesigned (its behavior is unchanged). each option is now a prominent emoji with a small label underneath, on one continuous frosted-glass surface with no borders or fills of their own, a soft press highlight, and a hairline between the two rows. row 1, object actions: duplicate (two overlapping 🟦, the front one lower and to the right), group 🫂, ungroup ✂️. row 2, stacking: to front ⏫, forward 🔼, backward 🔽, to back ⏬. options are 64×58px; each row centers only the actions that apply, so fewer actions make a shorter row with no empty slots. buttons keep their accessible names ("bring to front", "send backward", …) and stay keyboard focusable.
- `css/styles.css`: on narrow phones (under 400px wide) tool buttons are 41px instead of 44px so the seven tools still fit in one row.
- `js/main.js`: new projects start with a black canvas instead of white. existing projects keep their saved color.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: long presses and drags never select text or open the callout menu anywhere in moji (the search field and size fields still allow it), so holding an emoji on a phone no longer highlights the whole screen.
- `js/main.js`: each emoji's visible ink is measured once from its pixels instead of trusting the font's reported box, which some browsers give as the full em square for color emoji. emoji are now drawn with their visible center on their position, so rotation turns around the middle of what you see, and selection outlines, hit testing and the shared outline of a multiple selection fit wide or short glyphs (fixes the offset outline around 🏔️). existing artwork may shift by a few pixels where a glyph's ink was off-center.

### removed

- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`: the decorative track of the closed timeline capsule (replaced by the live strip).

## 0.0.2 — 2026-11-25

refinements from testing 0.0.1 on an iphone (a fix for portrait layout drift, delete and placement controls moved into the tools panel, delete all, button name captions, a friendlier canvas panel), followed by editing existing artwork (selection, moving, resizing, rotating, multiple selection, duplication, grouping, stacking order) and an interactive timeline with playback and branching history.

### added

- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: a drop-down tab that grows down out of the tools panel under the active tool. while placing, it hangs under ➕ and shows the current emoji (tap it to change emoji); while deleting, it hangs under ➖ and holds 🧹 (delete all). tapping ➕ or ➖ again, tapping any other tool (🎯, ✅, ↩️, ↪️, or the other of ➕/➖), or collapsing 🛠️ folds it back up and ends the mode; the tab only ever belongs to the tool in use. tapping a dimmed tool changes nothing.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the tools panel and its drop-down tab are one continuous glass shape that genuinely morphs. each frame, one svg path is rebuilt from three springs (panel width, tab depth, tab position) and used as the glass clip (`clip-path: path()`), the soft shadow, and the hairline edge. the tab grows out of the panel's bottom edge with smooth inward curves where they meet, or rises straight into the panel's rounded end when it sits at the edge (under ➕); switching between ➕ and ➖ slides the tab across; collapsing folds the tab up before the panel narrows, and expanding widens the panel before the tab grows. reduced motion jumps straight to the final shape.
- `js/main.js`: delete all (🧹), which removes every emoji, inside and outside the canvas, as one step (`clear-objects` in the action history). ↩️ restores everything; it is saved and restored like other edits, and 🧹 is dimmed when there is nothing to delete.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: a short caption that names the button just tapped (for example "delete", "delete all", "undo", "change emoji") right next to it: just under a toolbar button (moved beside the drop-down tab when the tab is there), beside the tab's own button, and above ⏳. it fades after about a second, names the button only without explaining it, and is hidden from screen readers, which use the buttons' own labels.
- `css/styles.css`: `--color-accent` (warm emoji yellow, `#FFD43B`) for primary actions, and `--font-family-numeric` (the system's rounded face, sf rounded on apple devices, the system ui face elsewhere) for numbers typed into fields.

- `js/history.js` (new): the history engine, separate from the interface. each branch is an ordered log of events (an edit, an undo, or a redo), so undo and redo stay visible as part of the creative process. the artwork and its undo/redo stacks at any moment are rebuilt by replaying events from the project's base state, with in-memory snapshots every 32 events for fast scrubbing (never saved). a branch stores only its own events plus the point it started from, so shared history is not copied. operations: `add-objects`, `delete-objects`, `transform-objects`, `set-groups`, `reorder`, plus the 0.0.1 types (`add-object`, `delete-object`, `clear-objects`, `resize-canvas`) so older projects replay unchanged. it also handles validation, saving, migration and the before/after transitions used by playback.
- `js/main.js`: selection as the default canvas behavior whenever ➕ and ➖ are off, with no selection button. tapping an emoji selects it (nothing moves, stacking is unchanged) and shows a subtle dashed outline that follows its rotated bounds; tapping empty space deselects; tapping another emoji switches. repeated taps on about the same spot (within 18px) cycle through overlapping emoji from the top down and back to the top. hit testing accounts for position, size, rotation, zoom, pan and stacking order, uses the same stage coordinates as rendering, and works for emoji outside the canvas.
- `js/main.js`: editing. a long press (about 0.4s) on any emoji selects it and starts editing at once; keep moving to drag it. once editing, dragging is immediate and releasing keeps editing on. editing ends when you tap empty space, select another emoji, or switch to ➕ or ➖.
- `js/main.js`: uniform resizing and rotation of the selection: a corner handle resizes, a handle above the outline rotates, and two fingers on the selection resize and rotate together. handles have 44px touch targets and the outline never draws smaller than 44px on screen, so tiny emoji stay manageable without changing their size. each completed drag, resize or rotation is one undoable `transform-objects` step; intermediate movement is not recorded.
- `js/main.js`: multiple selection without a selection tool. desktop: shift-click adds or removes an emoji; a plain click selects one; clicking empty space clears. touch: rest one finger on empty space and tap emoji with another finger to add or remove them; lifting the first finger keeps the selection. two fingers that move together still pan and zoom. a multiple selection shares one outline; a long press on any selected emoji edits all of them, and dragging, resizing and rotating act on the whole selection around its shared center while keeping each emoji's relative position, size and rotation.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: an object menu (compact frosted glass, near the selection, kept inside the screen) that opens when you tap the selection while editing; it never opens on its own. it shows only actions that would do something: duplicate, group, ungroup, to front, forward, backward, to back. opening 🛠️ or tapping elsewhere closes it without clearing the selection.
- `js/main.js`: duplicate copies the selected emoji with new ids, a small offset, and all other properties, placed directly above the originals (grouped originals become a new group).
- `js/main.js`: groups. grouping gives the selected emoji a shared `groupId`; selecting any member selects the whole group, which edits as one unit while each emoji keeps its own properties. ungrouping removes the `groupId` and leaves positions, sizes, rotations and stacking unchanged.
- `js/main.js`: stacking order: bring forward and send backward move the selection one step (another group counts as one step), bring to front and send to back move it to the top or bottom. coordinates, size and rotation never change, and the order is used for drawing, hit testing and export.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: an interactive timeline. ⏳ grows into a panel with a status line, a scrubber (a tick per event, undo and redo in amber, green dots where this branch started from another), ▶️ play/⏸️ pause, a speed button (0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×), 🌱 start from here, and 🌿 view branches. while open it takes priority: placing, deleting, editing and the object menu are suspended, outlines and handles hide, 🛠️ and the canvas label rest dimmed, and canvas taps never change the artwork (zoom and pan still work). closing returns to the latest state of the active branch and restores the previous mode and selection where they still exist.
- `js/main.js`: scrubbing rebuilds the artwork at any moment instantly (pointer or arrow keys) without touching the live artwork, which is what gets saved and exported.
- `js/main.js`: playback replays the active branch's whole process, mistakes and corrections included, at a steady rhythm. each step interpolates only what that event changed: new emoji fade and grow in, removed emoji fade out, moved, resized and rotated emoji travel smoothly, and canvas size changes animate; everything else stays still. pausing finishes the current step. reduced motion shows each step without animation.
- `js/main.js`: branching. 🌱 appears while previewing an earlier moment and starts a new branch there (named branch 1, branch 2, …), which becomes active with the selection cleared; the original branch and its later history are kept. 🌿 lists branches to switch (restoring that branch's latest artwork), rename (names persist), or delete (with a confirmation; the active branch and the last branch cannot be deleted). up to five branches per project; a sixth is refused with an explanation and nothing is deleted automatically. deleting a branch that others started from hands its shared history to them, so they stay intact.

### changed

- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: the bottom-right mode bar (➕ ✖️ while placing, ✖️ while deleting) is removed; every tool now lives in the top-right panel.
- `js/main.js`: ➕ no longer collapses the tools panel when opening the picker, choosing an emoji expands the panel so its drop-down tab is visible, and tapping ➕ while placing stops placing.
- `css/styles.css`: the canvas panel's width and height numbers use the rounded numeric face instead of atkinson hyperlegible, and apply is a warm yellow pill instead of near-black.
- `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`: while the drop-down tab overlaps the `canvas` label (on narrow screens), the label fades out and returns when the tab folds away; it never moves.
- `js/main.js`, `js/history.js`: autosave now stores the whole project (all branches with their events and names, the active branch, the project base, plus a safety copy of the current artwork) and never a timeline preview. on startup the project is rebuilt by replaying history and must reproduce the safety copy; if history cannot be read, the artwork is kept, the raw data is backed up under `moji.project.unrestored`, and a new history starts with a message. if storage fills up, saving pauses with a message instead of dropping branches or history.
- `js/history.js`: 0.0.1 projects are migrated on first load. the original data is kept under `moji.project.v1-backup`; its edits become the original branch, followed by one undo per edit beyond the saved cursor, which reproduces the same artwork and the same undo/redo stacks. 0.0.1 did not record when undos happened, so imported history shows each saved edit once. a 0.0.1 project saved without history keeps its artwork as the starting point.
- `js/main.js`: the 0.0.1 undo history (one list and a cursor) is replaced by the branch event log; ↩️ and ↪️ behave as before and are recorded as events.
- `js/main.js`: objects are never changed in place; every edit builds new objects, so history snapshots can share them safely.
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`: ⏳ is now a working control (previously a placeholder).

### fixed

- `js/main.js`: on iphone in portrait (found in firefox; every ios browser uses apple's webkit engine), the canvas, the canvas label, placed emoji, taps, and zoom could drift apart vertically and emoji could look stretched. the stage's pixel buffer was only resized on window `resize` events, but its height follows the dynamic viewport (`100dvh`), which changes as the browser toolbars show and hide without always firing `resize`. the stage now tracks its own size with a `ResizeObserver` (plus `visualViewport` and orientation events) and re-checks its size before every frame, so the buffer always matches what is on screen.
- `js/main.js`: touch, mouse, and wheel positions are now measured relative to the stage itself rather than the window.
- `css/styles.css`: ➖ no longer keeps a pressed circle while delete mode is on. the circle nearly filled the 48px toolbar, so it looked off-center, and it was the only button with a lasting highlight. ➕ and ➖ show their mode through the drop-down tab instead.

## 0.0.1 — 2026-11-23

initial visual interface, extended into a functional editor: canvas, zoom and pan, emoji picker and placement, delete, undo/redo, recenter, png export, and automatic local saving.

### added

- `index.html`: document structure with mobile viewport settings (`viewport-fit=cover`), page title, and a visually hidden `moji` heading.
- `index.html`: Atkinson Hyperlegible (regular and bold) loaded from Google Fonts, with a system sans-serif fallback.
- `index.html`: workspace layer and a `<canvas>` stage drawn by `js/main.js`; `js/emoji-data.js` and `js/main.js` load in that order with `defer`; theme color set to the workspace gray.
- `index.html`: settings button (upper left) with a ⚙️ emoji icon and a right-pointing chevron suggesting future rightward expansion.
- `index.html`: tools button (upper right) with 🛠️ and a small chevron, expanding into a row of ➕ add, ➖ delete, ↩️ undo, ↪️ redo, 🎯 recenter, and ✅ export.

- `index.html`: `canvas` label (frosted glass, text) that follows the canvas's upper-left edge at a constant screen size.

- `js/main.js`: document model in which every placed emoji is an independent object (id, emoji, render style, position in artwork coordinates, size, rotation, creation order, timestamp); array order is the stacking order. coordinates are centered on the canvas so canvas resizing never moves objects.
- `js/main.js`: action history of immutable, self-contained entries (`add-object`, `delete-object`, `resize-canvas`) that can each be applied and reverted; undo and redo move a cursor through the list, and a new action discards the redo branch.
- `js/main.js`: default 9:16 canvas (1080 × 1920 artwork px, `#FFFFFF`), fitted and centered between the top and bottom controls, with edges snapped to device pixels.
- `js/main.js`: zoom and pan that change only the view, never the artwork. touch: one finger taps, two fingers pan, pinch zooms. desktop: drag (left or middle button) pans; mouse wheel, trackpad scrolling, and trackpad pinch all zoom around the pointer. every wheel event zooms, instead of guessing whether it came from a mouse or a trackpad.
- `js/main.js`: stage rendering that redraws the white canvas and all objects from the document model each frame at the device pixel ratio, using the platform's native color emoji font; objects outside the canvas stay visible on the workspace.
- `js/main.js`: workspace grid kept aligned with the artwork while zooming and panning, with spacing adapting to stay visually quiet.
- `js/main.js`: tap detection that ignores pinches, multi-finger gestures, and drags, so gestures never place or delete emoji.
- `js/main.js`: tools panel behavior: 🛠️ toggles the panel; ➕ closes it and opens the picker; ➖ toggles delete mode and keeps the panel open; ↩️ and ↪️ undo and redo without closing the panel (dimmed when unavailable); 🎯 and ✅ act immediately without changing the current mode.
- `js/main.js`: 🎯 recenter, which smoothly returns the view to the fitted, centered canvas (instantly when reduced motion is preferred); it changes only the view and creates no history entry.
- `js/emoji-data.js`: the emoji catalog (1,369 emoji in nine categories, each with a searchable name), kept as a data-only file separate from the application logic in `js/main.js`; `js/main.js` reads it from `window.MOJI_EMOJI_CATEGORIES`.
- `js/main.js`: emoji picker with search (by unicode name), category navigation that follows scrolling, and a scrollable grid of 1,369 emoji (unicode 13.0 and earlier) across nine categories; selecting an emoji closes the picker and enters placement mode without placing anything.
- `js/main.js`: placement mode, where each tap places a new copy of the selected emoji at the tapped artwork position.
- `js/main.js`: delete mode, where each tap removes only the topmost emoji under the pointer (with a minimum touch radius for small emoji).
- `js/main.js`: canvas properties opened from the canvas label: aspect ratio presets (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 3:4, 16:9) and custom width and height (64–4096 px); resizing is symmetric around the center, undoable, and never moves, scales, rotates, or removes objects. the popover opens above the label when there is room.
- `js/main.js`: one-tap png export: ✅ renders the png from the document model at the canvas's own pixel size, independent of zoom, and starts the browser's download directly, with no preview or dialog. only the canvas area is drawn, so the grid, interface, label, and objects outside the canvas are excluded and objects crossing the edge are cropped. failures show a brief message.
- `js/main.js`: automatic local saving of the structured project (canvas, objects, and undo/redo history) to `localStorage`, about 0.4s after each change (placement, deletion, canvas resize, undo, redo) and when the page is hidden. if storage is full, the artwork is saved without history.
- `js/main.js`: automatic restore on startup, validated before use and without creating history entries; damaged history is dropped while the artwork is kept. a project that cannot be restored is copied to a backup key (`moji.project.unrestored`) before anything new is saved; if no backup can be made, autosave pauses for the session.
- `js/main.js`: escape key closes an open panel, then exits the current mode, then collapses the tools panel; ios page-zoom gestures and the stage context menu are suppressed.
